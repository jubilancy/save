# ✂️ Castration

Codex:

[Castration Anxiety](https://memeanalysis.webflow.io/codex)

Castration based fetishes are the most common, as they are rooted in the body.

Freud theorized fetishism was rooted in Castration Anxiety, or the realization that women lacked the phallus, the unconscious would divert this phallic libido to another part of the body.

> _Yes, in his mind the woman has got a penis, in spite of everything; but this penis is no longer the same as it was before. Something else has taken its place, has been appointed its substitute, as it were, and now inherits the interest which was formerly directed to its predecessor. But this interest suffers an extraordinary increase as well, because the horror of castration has set up a memorial to itself in the creation of this substitute._

FREUD](https://memeanalysis.webflow.io/#)

# 👨‍👩‍👦 Oedipus

Codex:

[Oedipus](https://memeanalysis.webflow.io/codex)

Oedipal fetishes differ from Castration based fetishes in that they are rooted in family dynamics and complexes. Libido is no longer diverted into a part of the body or object, but into a power dynamic.

Examples:

‍

\- BDSM

\- Femdom

\- ABDL

# 📺 Media

Codex:

[Media Induced](https://memeanalysis.webflow.io/codex)

Media induced fetishism has its root in entertainment, rather than a lived experience. A _virtual Oedipus_. Your libido is directed into the virtual and away from the body and relationships.

Examples:

‍

\- Quicksand

\- Transformations

\- Cartoonish scenarios

# 🖥 Porn

Codex:

[Porn Induced](https://memeanalysis.webflow.io/codex)

Porn induced fetishes bear no relation to the body, but are formed through prolonged use of porn. A _virtual phallus_. Your libido has been directed completely into the virtual.

Examples:

‍

\- Extremely exaggerated body parts

\- Physically impossible scenarios
