## The Internet

The Wired, the interconnected circuitous energies of humanity. An energetic diorama of the real world

[The Internet](https://memeanalysis.webflow.io/extraterrestrial#)

Someone who has fallen down a [rabbit hole](https://www.notion.so/Hole-578be1ac69da4f0fbac43621e39e0659) and reached the bitter end.

This can be a long process, or extremely quick.

They are cephalic, their [bodies](https://www.notion.so/Body-6aa0e55bc94049bfbf3e31f92bbf9b9e) are withered ( [food](https://www.notion.so/33475fc822534b2d83a949fcd23caa9a) for [monsters](https://www.notion.so/Entities-e615f1e59aa04aef8a3e6f13520552f8)), they identify entirely with an online [persona](https://www.notion.so/b8bc6ca28147406bb258be4fc2d7917c).

These are the _lolcows_ who act as blood bags for [trolls](https://www.notion.so/Satyr-9d2cfc5d55184e2485d6ed0f34518fb1), so far gone, they are bizarre and amusing.

The irony is that trolls are in the process of becoming-alien, and mock lolcows because they are embodiments of what's coming for [them](https://www.notion.so/e2ba5683d69547a3b5e9c30cab11648e).
